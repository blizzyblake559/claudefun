# Aura: A Teaching Curriculum

**Based on "Mystique: A Field Manual" — research synthesis on the cultivation of durable fascination, presence, and social gravity.**

This curriculum is for someone who already has the underlying assets (competence, style, social capital) and wants to understand the *discipline* of maintaining and compounding them. It is not about acquisition. It is about the harder, rarer skill: not leaking what you have.

## The Central Thesis in Three Sentences

Mystique is not hiding. It is a **moderate, unresolved, positively-charged knowledge gap** — visible enough to be intriguing, opaque enough to stay unresolved. Everything in this curriculum is a method for engineering and protecting that gap.

---

## Evidence Honesty

This material draws on peer-reviewed research, historical analysis, and expert synthesis. Where claims are contested or merely inferred, they are flagged. The goal is a working model grounded in evidence, not a collection of compelling-sounding assertions.
