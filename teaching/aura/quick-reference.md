# Quick Reference — Aura Field Card

*Print this. Keep it. Use it before you walk into anything that matters.*

---

## The Central Equation

```
Mystique = Moderate, unresolved, positively-charged knowledge gap
         = High visibility / Low legibility
```

---

## The 8 Principles (condensed)

| # | Principle | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visibility / Legibility | Be seen constantly, explained never |
| 2 | Glamour = concealment + distance | Hide the effort; leave imaginative space |
| 3 | Scarcity from demand | Hard to reach because full, not hiding |
| 4 | Show dominant, conceal submissive | Reappraise, don't suppress |
| 5 | Bet on appreciating assets | Prestige and network over dominance |
| 6 | Deep to few, withheld from many | Intense to 5; gap-preserving to 500 |
| 7 | Stay an unfinished story | Always a next chapter; never narrate the destination |
| 8 | Real posture, real life | Technique is downstream of the life |

---

## In-Person Check (before you enter)

- [ ] Do I have a natural exit time in mind?
- [ ] Am I arriving where outcomes matter, not just drifting?
- [ ] Am I genuinely interested in these people — or performing interest?

**While there:** Show less than you feel (both ends). Under-react. Amused, not aroused.

**When leaving:** Leave early, leave while it's still good, leave an open loop.

---

## Online Check (before you post)

1. Does this **open** a gap or **close** one?
2. Is this image/motion/craft — or explanation/opinion/feeling?
3. Does this caption explain, justify, or narrate logistics?
4. Does this post acknowledge that an audience exists?

**Hard rules:** Never react online. No clapbacks, no defenses, no vaguebooking. Wealth as set dressing only, never as subject.

## The Four Leaks (self-monitor weekly)

| Leak | Recognition | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure | Everywhere, always available | Audit quarterly; leave early |
| Disclosure | Explaining unprompted, confessional posting | Fragment to many; reserve depth for 5 |
| Neediness | Over-following-up, engineering proximity | Build the life; let things come |
| Legibility | Reacting when stillness serves better | Amused, not aroused; never react online |

---

## Under Pressure

| Pressure | Response |
|---|---|
| Public provocation / dig | Amused, unhurried, on your own clock. Never respond online. |
| Pull to explain yourself | Don't. Brevity, warmth, or a question back. |
| Disclosure trap ("what's really going on") | Warm, gap-preserving fragment. Not the full file. |
| Envy / cut-down | Composure is the defense. Scarcity and calm are envy-resistant. |
| Reachability creep | Audit quarterly. Scarcity decays by default. |

```
INVEST (appreciating)          MAINTAIN (slowable depreciation)
├── Real competence            └── Physical base
├── Reputation (no leaks)
└── Network bridges

STOP BUILDING (depreciating)
├── Dominance signaling
└── Overexposure
```

## The Foundation Check (once per quarter)

- Do I have at least two autotelic pursuits I engage in regularly that aren't for an audience?
- Am I comfortable with chosen solitude?
- Is status a byproduct of my life — or the organizing principle of it?
- Does my inner circle get the real, unguarded version of me?

If the answer to the last question is no: the discipline elsewhere is performance, and performance cracks.
